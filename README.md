# Rube Goldberg Machine — Physics Mechanics Prototype

Multi-stage mechanical chain-reaction system designed, built, and tested for the Physics Mechanics course and presented at the **ExposanBuena 2026-II** academic showcase at Universidad de San Buenaventura (Bogotá).

## Physical Principles Applied
- **Kinematics & Uniformly Accelerated Motion:** Design of inclined planes, ramps, and drop trajectories to control timing and velocity between stages.
- **Conservation of Mechanical Energy:** Conversion between gravitational potential energy and kinetic energy across sequential triggers.
- **Linear Momentum & Collisions:** Calibrated impact transfers between rolling spheres, levers, and sequential mechanisms.
